**Last updated:** December 11, 2025

## Overview

Crochetti ("the App") is a crochet pattern tracking application for Android. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how the App handles your information.

## Data Collection

**We do not collect any personal data.** The App operates entirely offline and does not transmit any information to external servers.

## Local Storage

All data you create in the App—including projects, patterns, and preferences—is stored locally on your device. This data:

- Never leaves your device
- Is not accessible to us or any third parties
- Is not backed up to any cloud service by the App

If you uninstall the App, all locally stored data will be deleted.

## Camera Permission

The App requests camera permission **only** for the QR code scanning feature. This permission is:

- **Optional** — The App functions fully without it
- **Used only for QR code scanning** — No photos are taken or stored
- **Never transmitted** — Camera data is processed locally and immediately discarded

You can deny or revoke this permission at any time through your device settings.

## Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children. Since no data is collected from any user, the App is safe for users of all ages.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

## Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us by opening an issue on our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/ChristopherJMiller/crochetti).
